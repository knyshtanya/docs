Privacy Policy for Wordlio

Last updated: July 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wordlio, an Android application developed by Tatsiana Knysh, handles information when you use the app.

Information handled by Wordlio

Wordlio does not require user registration and does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

Your learning progress and app preferences may be stored locally on your device to provide the app's functionality. This locally stored information is not transmitted to the developer. You can delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Advertising

Wordlio uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, general location derived from the IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and information about advertisements shown to you. This information may be used to provide, measure, personalize, and prevent fraud in advertising, subject to your consent where required by law.

For more information about how Google handles data, please see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your privacy choices

Where required, Wordlio may present a consent form that allows you to choose whether advertising may be personalized. You may also be able to change your privacy choices from the app's settings. You can manage or reset the Android advertising identifier through your device's privacy settings.

Data sharing

The developer does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by Google AdMob and its advertising partners as described above. The developer may also disclose information when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

Data security

Wordlio is designed to minimize the information handled by the developer. Local app data is protected by the security features of your Android device. Information processed by third-party services is handled under their respective security practices and privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

The developer does not maintain user accounts or a database containing your learning progress. Locally stored progress and preferences remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall Wordlio. Retention of information processed by Google is governed by Google's policies and your account or device settings.

Children's privacy

Wordlio is not directed specifically to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, please contact the developer so that appropriate action can be taken.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Wordlio's features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page, and the “Last updated” date will be revised.
