Privacy Policy for Wordlio

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wordlio, an Android application developed by Tatsiana Knysh, handles information when you use the app.

Information handled by Wordlio

Wordlio does not require user registration and does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, or precise location.

Your vocabulary, learning progress, review history, installed word packs, notification settings, purchase status, and other app preferences are stored locally on your device to provide the app's functionality.

Wordlio does not operate a developer-controlled account system or database containing your vocabulary or learning progress.

Translation service

When you request a translation, the word or text you submit is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to a Cloudflare Worker operated for Wordlio. The Worker forwards the text to DeepL to generate the translation.

When you use the image scanning feature, individual words recognized from an image may also be sent to the Wordlio Cloudflare Worker for spelling completion and to DeepL for translation.

Do not submit confidential, sensitive, or personally identifiable information for translation. The developer does not intentionally store submitted translation text in a developer-controlled database. However, Cloudflare and DeepL may process technical information and service request data according to their own terms, security practices, and privacy policies.

For more information, see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and the DeepL Privacy Policy .

Camera, images, and text recognition

Wordlio may request access to your device's camera or allow you to select an image so that text can be recognized and added to your vocabulary.

Image text recognition is performed on the device using Google ML Kit. Wordlio does not upload or store the original image on a developer-controlled server. Recognized words may be sent to the translation service as described above.

Camera access is used only when you choose to use the scanning feature. You can deny or revoke camera permission through Android settings.

Microphone and voice input

Wordlio may request microphone access when you choose to enter a word using your voice. Voice recognition is provided by the speech recognition service available on your Android device.

Depending on your device, Android version, speech service provider, and settings, audio or recognized speech may be processed by Google or another speech service provider. Wordlio does not store microphone recordings on a developer-controlled server.

You can deny or revoke microphone permission through Android settings. For information about Google's handling of voice data, see the Google Privacy Policy .

Advertising

The free version of Wordlio uses Google AdMob to display banner and rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, general location derived from the IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and information about advertisements shown to you.

This information may be used to provide, measure, personalize, and prevent fraud in advertising, subject to your consent where required by law.

For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy .

Your advertising privacy choices

Where required by law, Wordlio presents a Google consent form that allows you to choose how your information may be used for advertising, including whether advertisements may be personalized.

When required, you can review or change your advertising privacy choices through the privacy options available in Wordlio's settings. You can also manage or reset the Android advertising identifier through your device's privacy settings.

Purchases

Wordlio offers an optional Premium purchase through Google Play. Purchases and payment information are processed by Google Play Billing. Wordlio does not receive or store your full payment card or bank account details.

Wordlio receives purchase status information from Google Play so that it can activate or restore Premium features. Purchase processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play terms.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Wordlio stores your selected reminder times locally and uses Android's notification and background scheduling features to display learning reminders.

Wordlio does not use a developer-controlled server to send these reminders. You can disable reminders in Wordlio or revoke notification permission through Android settings.

Import and export

Wordlio allows you to import and export vocabulary files. Files are accessed or created only when you initiate the corresponding action and select a location through Android's system file interface.

Exported files may contain vocabulary and learning information. You are responsible for choosing where these files are stored and with whom they are shared.

Android backup

Depending on your device and Android backup settings, locally stored Wordlio data may be included in an Android system backup associated with your device or Google account.

These backups are managed by Android and the applicable backup provider, not by the Wordlio developer. You can manage backup settings through your device settings.

Data sharing

The developer does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers only as needed to provide app functionality, including:

  • Google AdMob for advertising;
  • Google Play for purchases;
  • Google or another device speech service for voice recognition;
  • Cloudflare for routing translation requests;
  • DeepL for generating translations.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

Data security

Wordlio is designed to minimize the information handled by the developer. Local app data is protected by the security features of your Android device.

Translation requests are transmitted using HTTPS. However, no method of electronic transmission, processing, or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Information processed by third-party services is handled according to their respective security practices and privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

The developer does not maintain Wordlio user accounts or a developer-controlled database containing your vocabulary or learning progress.

Locally stored vocabulary, progress, review history, and preferences remain on your device until you delete them in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall Wordlio. Android backups may remain subject to your backup provider's retention rules.

Information processed by Google, Cloudflare, DeepL, or another service provider is retained according to that provider's policies and your applicable account or device settings.

Children's privacy

Wordlio is not directed specifically to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through Wordlio, please contact the developer so that the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action can be taken.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Wordlio's features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page, and the “Last updated” date will be revised.
